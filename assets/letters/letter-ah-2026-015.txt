SUBJECT: 
Immediate Action on Illegal Commercial Backyard Breeding & Unregulated Pet Shops 
Violating the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (Dog Breeding and Marketing) Rules.

Respected Sir,

VOKAL submits herewith an investigative docket tracking 43 illegal, un-registered commercial dog breeding units operating inside Kerala. Female dogs are subjected to continuous forced pregnancies in cramped, dark cages without veterinary care or social interaction, only to be dumped on roadsides when sick or infertile.

WE URGE:
1. Constitution of Special Task Forces involving Animal Husbandry, Police, and SPCA officials to inspect all pet establishments.
2. Immediate confiscation and medical rehabilitation of abused animals.
3. Strict enforcement of registration requirements with the Kerala State Animal Welfare Board.

Sincerely,

Investigation & Legal Cell, VOKAL
