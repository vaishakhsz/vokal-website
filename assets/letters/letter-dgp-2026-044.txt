SUBJECT: 
Representation Requesting Mandatory Police Circular for Compulsory Registration of FIRs 
in Animal Cruelty Cases under PCA Act 1960 and IPC 428/429 / BNS 325.

Respected Sir,

VOKAL respectfully places before you a serious procedural lapse occurring across several police stations in the State. Citizens reporting heinous animal cruelty (including beatings, hangings, intentional vehicular runs, and poisoning) are repeatedly turned away by Station House Officers (SHOs) under the misconception that cruelty complaints are non-cognizable or of low priority.

Cruelty to animals is frequently correlated by criminologists with escalating violence against vulnerable human beings (women, children, and elderly). The law explicitly provides that offences under IPC Section 429 / BNS 325 are cognizable, non-bailable, and warrant prompt registration of an FIR.

WE RESPECTFULLY PRAY FOR THE FOLLOWING INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Issue an executive circular to all Station House Officers across Kerala mandating prompt registration of FIRs upon receipt of animal cruelty complaints.
2. Designate a trained Animal Welfare Nodal Officer of Sub-Inspector rank in each District Police Office.
3. Establish a standard protocol for veterinary autopsy and forensic preservation in poisoning and blunt trauma deaths.

Warm regards,

Legal Cell Convener, VOKAL
