SUBJECT: 
Urgent Memorandum for Full Implementation of Animal Birth Control (ABC) Rules 2023, 
Allocation of Taluk-Level Veterinary Infrastructure, and Immediate Action Against Vigilante Cruelty.

Respected Sir,

We, on behalf of VOKAL (Voice of Kerala for Animal Legit) and thousands of animal caretakers, veterinary doctors, and law-abiding citizens across the state of Kerala, submit this urgent representation regarding the humane and scientific management of community animals.

Under Article 51A(g) of the Constitution of India, it is the fundamental duty of every citizen of India to have compassion for living creatures. Furthermore, the Supreme Court of India in Animal Welfare Board of India v. A. Nagaraja (2014) 7 SCC 547 recognized that animals have intrinsic rights to life and dignity under Article 21.

Recent reports of vigilante poisonings and unlawful culling in various panchayats violate the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (PCA) Act, 1960 and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita / Indian Penal Code Section 429. Scientific epidemiology clearly proves that mass culling causes a severe vacuum effect, attracting unvaccinated outside dogs and escalating human-animal conflicts. The ONLY scientifically validated solution is the strict, high-volume implementation of the Animal Birth Control (ABC) & Anti-Rabies Vaccination (ARV) Rules 2023.

OUR KEY DEMANDS:
1. Operationalize state-of-the-art, accredited ABC centers in every Taluk equipped with CCTV surveillance, licensed veterinary surgeons, and pre-and-post operative kennels.
2. Issue strict directives to District Collectors and Police Commissioners to arrest and prosecute any individual or organized syndicate spreading poisoned meat or issuing bounties on stray dogs.
3. Establish 24x7 Government Veterinary Casualty and Emergency Trauma Units in all Municipal Corporations.
4. Partner transparently with recognized AWBI animal welfare organizations and registered feeder networks.

We eagerly anticipate your urgent intervention to restore constitutional justice and scientific governance in God's Own Country.

Yours faithfully,

General Secretary, VOKAL
