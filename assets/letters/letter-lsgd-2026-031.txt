SUBJECT: 
Enforcement of Feeder Protection and Demarcation of Designated Community Animal 
Feeding Zones in All Municipalities and Corporations across Kerala.

Respected Madam,

The High Court of Kerala and the Supreme Court of India have repeatedly held that feeding stray animals is a lawful activity protected under the Constitution of India. Stray dogs cannot be starved; hungry dogs become anxious and territory-defensive, which directly increases human-dog bite occurrences. Feeding community animals makes them docile, facilitates easy vaccination, and ensures community safety.

However, compassionate citizens, particularly elderly women and students, regularly face severe harassment, verbal abuse, and physical intimidation by resident associations and ward members when feeding animals.

WE REQUEST THE LSGD TO:
1. Issue clear circulars to all Municipal Commissioners and Panchayat Secretaries upholding citizen feeder rights.
2. Demarcate hygienic community feeding points away from busy pedestrian intersections and school gates.
3. Install informational signboards informing the public that harassing animal feeders is a punishable offence.

Thank you.

Director of Community Outreach, VOKAL
