Guiding Principles

Our Vision & Mission

Anchored in constitutional jurisprudence and universal moral ethics, VOKAL works tirelessly to make Kerala a sanctuary of peaceful human-animal coexistence.

The VOKAL Vision

"To establish a Kerala where every animal is recognized with inherent dignity, legal legitimacy, and constitutional protection under Article 51A(g), creating a civilized society where humans and animals live side by side free from culling, cruelty, hunger, and terror."

Legal Enforcement

Direct legal action against cruelty, moving High Court petitions, ensuring police FIR registration under IPC 428/429 and PCA Act 1960.

Scientific ABC & ARV

Enforcing the ABC Rules 2023 for humane sterilization and massive anti-rabies vaccination across all corporations and grama panchayats.

Feeder Protection

Defending compassionate caretakers from unlawful harassment, issuing certified identity cards, and setting up hygienic community feeding points.

Humane Education

Conducting educational drives in schools and colleges to foster kindness, bite-prevention literacy, and empathy among Kerala's younger generation.
