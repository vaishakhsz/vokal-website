Guiding Principles

Our Vision & Mission

Anchored in constitutional jurisprudence and universal moral ethics, VOKAL works tirelessly to make Kerala a sanctuary of peaceful human-animal coexistence.

The VOKAL Vision

"To foster a world where humans, animals, and nature coexist in harmony within a protected, sustainable, and thriving ecosystem."

Ground Action Plan

The VOKAL Mission

To protect animals, conserve vital water resources, and restore natural environments by empowering local communities, advocating for humane animal welfare, and driving impactful, grassroots conservation.

Legal Enforcement

Direct legal action against cruelty, moving High Court petitions, ensuring police FIR registration under IPC 428/429 and PCA Act 1960.

Scientific ABC & ARV

Enforcing the ABC Rules 2023 for humane sterilization and massive anti-rabies vaccination across all corporations and grama panchayats.

Feeder Protection

Defending compassionate caretakers from unlawful harassment, issuing certified identity cards, and setting up hygienic community feeding points.

Humane Education

Conducting educational drives in schools and colleges to foster kindness, bite-prevention literacy, and empathy among Kerala's younger generation.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Compassion in Action

Every creature deserves kindness. We act — not just advocate — for the voiceless among us.

Environmental Stewardship

Protecting nature, water, and ecosystems is inseparable from protecting life itself.

Community Empowerment

Change starts at the grassroots. We equip communities, feeders, and youth to be agents of transformation.

Integrity & Interconnectedness

We act with transparency and recognize that the well-being of humans, animals, and nature are deeply interlinked.

VOKAL actively engages schools and colleges across Kerala, nurturing the next generation of compassionate leaders who understand that a just society is one that protects its most vulnerable — both human and non-human.

Environment

Protecting Nature & Environment

VOKAL is deeply committed to the preservation of Kerala's rich natural heritage — its forests, wetlands, rivers, and biodiversity. Our environmental protection initiatives focus on raising public awareness, supporting reforestation efforts, and opposing activities that threaten Kerala's fragile ecosystems. We believe that a healthy natural environment is the foundation upon which all life thrives.

By working alongside local communities, tribal groups, and environmental experts, VOKAL advocates for stronger environmental protections, sustainable land use, and the enforcement of existing environmental laws. Our mission is to ensure that future generations inherit a Kerala that is green, clean, and ecologically vibrant.

Reforestation Ecosystem Protection Sustainability Advocacy
Water Resources

Preserving & Protecting Water Resources

Kerala, the land of rivers, lakes, and backwaters, faces increasing threats to its water bodies from pollution, encroachment, and over-exploitation. VOKAL champions the cause of water conservation by collaborating with authorities, engaging local communities, and leading clean-up drives in rivers, ponds, and wetlands across all 14 districts.

We advocate for the protection of Kerala's rivers and drinking water sources, which are vital not just for humans but for wildlife and aquatic ecosystems too. Ensuring clean water is a matter of justice — for every living being who depends on it.

Community

People's Empowerment

At the heart of VOKAL's work is an unwavering belief in the power of people. We empower individuals, families, and communities across Kerala to become active participants in animal welfare, environmental conservation, and social justice.

Through training programs, community workshops, legal literacy sessions, and feeder networks, VOKAL builds the capacity of ordinary citizens to make an extraordinary difference. Our grassroots approach ensures that the voices of the most marginalized — whether they be vulnerable feeders, tribal communities, or animal welfare advocates — are heard and respected.

Feeder ID Networks Legal Literacy Youth Outreach
Animal Welfare

Animal Compassion

Every stray dog on the street, every injured bird on the roadside, every cow abandoned by its owner — each of these creatures deserves dignity, care, and protection under the law. VOKAL's Animal Compassion programs combine emergency rescue, veterinary care, legal intervention, and community education to create a Kerala where no animal is left to suffer alone.

Guided by the teachings of great spiritual traditions and the constitutional mandate of Article 51A(g), VOKAL mobilizes volunteers, veterinarians, and legal experts to respond to cruelty, support injured animals, and build lasting systems of compassion across every district in Kerala.
